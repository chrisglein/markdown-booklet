## 2. Padding

Booklets are built from folded sheets, so the total page count must be a
multiple of four. When the content does not divide evenly, blank pages are
appended until it does.

This sampler did not start life as a tidy multiple of four. The blank pages
you may have flipped past — and any blank trailing this chapter — were
inserted automatically: some to satisfy *start-on-recto* requests, the last
ones simply to fill out the final sheet.

That is the whole bargain of explicit pagination: you decide where the
content breaks, and the imposer handles the arithmetic of the fold.
