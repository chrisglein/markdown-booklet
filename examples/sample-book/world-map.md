### The Imposition Map  *(left)*

For eight logical pages, a single signature imposes onto two sheets:

| Sheet | Front | Back |
|------:|:-----:|:----:|
| 1 | 8 · 1 | 2 · 7 |
| 2 | 6 · 3 | 4 · 5 |

The left column of every sheet side is a **verso** (even page); the right
column is a **recto** (odd page).

<!-- spread-break -->

### The Imposition Map  *(right)*

Only the centermost facing pair — here pages 4 and 5 — actually lands side by
side on the same physical sheet. Every other facing pair is split across
different leaves of the fold.

That is why a picture meant to span the gutter belongs at the **center** of
the signature, and why this spread is a useful thing to be able to declare
explicitly.
