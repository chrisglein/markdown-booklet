## Preface

A booklet is the simplest bound form: a stack of sheets folded once down the
middle and stitched at the spine. The trick is *imposition* — arranging the
logical pages onto physical sheets so that, once folded, they read in order.

This sampler exists to exercise the pipeline end to end:

- a raw-HTML **cover** and **copyright** page,
- markdown **chapters** that each begin on a right-hand page,
- a two-page **spread**, and
- automatically inserted **blank** pages where alignment requires them.

Turn the page to begin.
