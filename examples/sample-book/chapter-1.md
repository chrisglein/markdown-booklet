## 1. Folding

Take a single sheet of Letter paper and fold it once, short edge to short
edge. You now hold four pages: two on the front of the sheet, two on the
back. Number them as a reader would encounter them and you will find they are
*not* in printing order.

The outermost leaf carries the highest and lowest page numbers together. The
innermost leaf carries the two middle numbers, side by side. Everything in
between nests symmetrically.

> A booklet is a palindrome of paper: page 1 shares a sheet with the last
> page, page 2 with the second-to-last, and so on toward the center.
